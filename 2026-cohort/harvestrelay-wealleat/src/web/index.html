How it works

Every handoff is confirmed in person, so nothing gets lost along the way.

  1. A donor posts extra food

    They choose how it's stored (cold, hot, dry or frozen), list allergens, and set a pickup cutoff of up to two hours.

  2. A kitchen claims and collects it

    Only kitchens that can store it, have room, and can arrive in time see it. The donor checks the kitchen's PIN at pickup.

  3. Neighbors reserve a meal

    People reserve a few servings and show a code at the kitchen. Unclaimed holds go back to others after 45 minutes.
