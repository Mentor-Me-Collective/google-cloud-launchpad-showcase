## Set 1 · 2026-11-06 · picked Clean Operational, built
Brief: ClimateOps is a mobile app for facility managers in the South Bronx to monitor and update cooling centers during extreme heat events. The home screen presents an overview of active facilities with their operational status and vulnerable population metrics. Managers can quickly review key data points and assess readiness across all locations.
1. Clean Operational (picked): A crisp, high-contrast light theme using industrial blues and crisp white cards designed for quick readability outdoors.. design/sets/2026-11-06-1/1-clean-operational.jpg
2. Dark Emergency: A dark-mode aesthetic with amber and crimson warning accents optimised for low-light control room environments.. design/sets/2026-11-06-1/2-dark-emergency.jpg
3. Civic Editorial: A sophisticated municipal style featuring warm paper tones, serif headings, and structured data layouts.. design/sets/2026-11-06-1/3-civic-editorial.jpg
4. High-Vis Utility: A bold, utilitarian design using oversized typography and punchy color-coded badges for instant status checks.. design/sets/2026-11-06-1/4-high-vis-utility.jpg
