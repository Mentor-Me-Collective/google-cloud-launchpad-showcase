Slide 2 · Problem & Grounding

Extreme Heat Creates Fatal Coordination Friction

Why current weather alerts fail to protect vulnerable urban facility occupants.

1. Alert Lag

National Weather Service alerts are broadcast as generic CAP text. They lack specific building context, equipment status, or physical capacity.

2. Priority Paralysis

Operations teams balance senior centers, youth centers, and public libraries simultaneously without a transparent, explainable ranking of vulnerability.

3. Unaccountable Actions

Preparation checklists dispatched over group texts or emails lack cryptographic proof, photo verification, or an immutable audit trail.

Grounded in South Bronx Reality (Bronx CD1)

Mott Haven and Melrose suffer from $<7\%$ tree canopy, high impervious surface coverage, and up to $8.6^\circ\text{F}$ urban heat island excess. ClimateOps grounds 10 facilities (8 public verified via NYPL, NYC Parks, and BronxWorks + 2 synthetic test controls) across two distinct tenant workspaces.

Slide 3 · Architecture

End-to-End Enterprise Architecture

Google Cloud Serverless Infrastructure orchestrated with NVIDIA NIM Microservices.

Google Cloud Core Services

  • Cloud Run: Stateless auto-scaling FastAPI service with zero-cost cold starts.
  • Cloud Firestore: Multi-tenant isolation with native-mode immutable event sourcing.
  • Cloud Storage (GCS): Short-lived HMAC signed URLs for photo proof evidence.
  • Secret Manager: Zero hardcoded secrets in git; runtime key injection.

NVIDIA NIM AI Microservices

  • nvidia/nemotron-3.5-bolt-e4b: Formulates actionable building readiness action plans grounded strictly in verified building facts.
  • meta/llama-3.2-90b-vision-instruct: Multimodal validation of uploaded chiller gauge and AC inspection photos.
  • Deterministic Fallback: Zero-failover template engine guarantees 100% operational continuity if AI is offline.

Slide 5 · Safety Governance

ADR-001: The Human-in-the-Loop Approval Gate

Mandatory human manager certification before physical building tasks are dispatched.
"AI proposes structured recommendations, but an authorized human manager must certify every operational brief. The system cryptographically binds approval to the SHA-256 brief hash."

Role-Based Access (RBAC)

Field staff and validators are blocked from approving briefs. Attempted bypass returns strict HTTP 403 Forbidden.

Tamper Prevention

Attempting to generate tasks on an unapproved draft returns HTTP 409 Conflict. Editing a draft revokes prior approval.

Strict Citation Anchors

Every recommendation in the AI brief must link to a verified fact tag: [FACT-1], [FACT-2]. Unanchored claims are rejected.

Slide 6 · Execution & Verification

Field Proof & Immutable Audit Ledger

Zero-trust task execution from mobile photo upload to cryptographic audit stream.

Direct Cloud Storage Signed Uploads

Staff technicians upload photo proof directly to Google Cloud Storage using 15-minute expiring HMAC signed PUT URLs. Server memory remains lean and credentials never pass through the browser.

Firestore Native Event Sourcing

Every state change creates an append-only event record in Firestore with microsecond timestamps, actor IDs, content hashes, and GCS URIs. Provides municipal agencies with a tamper-evident record of pre-event heat mitigation.
